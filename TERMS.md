# Epic MCP: Terms of Use

_Last updated: November 15, 2026_

By authorizing Epic MCP to access your health records, you agree to these terms.

## 1. What Epic MCP is
Epic MCP is free, open-source software that you run on your own computer. With your permission, it reads your medication list, the prescribers of those medications, and basic demographics from your health system's patient portal (MyChart) through standard FHIR APIs. It then makes that information available to an AI assistant that you choose to connect it to.

## 2. Your data
- Epic MCP has **read-only** access. It cannot change your medical record.
- Epic MCP runs locally. **The developer does not operate any server and never receives, stores or sees your health data or login credentials.**
- Access tokens and any retrieved data are stored only on your own device.
- You decide which AI assistant receives your data. That assistant's own terms and privacy policy govern how it handles your information.
- See the [Privacy & Data Use Disclosure](PRIVACY.md) for details.

## 3. Revoking access
You can revoke Epic MCP's access at any time from your MyChart account, typically under "Linked Apps" or "Manage App Access." You can also delete Epic MCP's local token files or uninstall the software.

## 4. Not medical advice
Epic MCP and any AI assistant output are for informational purposes only. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always confirm medication information with your doctor or pharmacist. Do not stop, start or change a medication based on this software.
